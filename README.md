# Hermès Luxury Retail Analytics | Power BI

**Luxury Retail Business Intelligence & Performance Analysis**

## Project Overview

Hermès Luxury Retail Analytics is a multi-page Power BI dashboard project developed to analyze luxury retail business performance through interactive visualizations and business intelligence.

The project focuses on sales, profitability, customer activity, product performance, sales channels and business growth trends. It combines analytical reporting with a premium visual design inspired by Hermès' brand identity.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance using KPIs.
- Analyze sales and profit across different cities.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Evaluate sales performance across different channels.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Measure Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1: Hermès Luxury Retail Dashboard

- Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders and Total Customers.
- Total Cost analysis.
- City-wise sales performance.
- Top 5 products by sales.
- Sales by product category.
- Sales by channel.
- Profit margin percentage by product category.
- Monthly sales trend.
- Interactive filters for year, month, product category and city.

### Page 2: MoM and YoY Analysis

- Monthly sales performance.
- Previous-year sales comparison.
- Year-over-Year (YoY) growth percentage.
- Previous-month sales comparison.
- Month-over-Month (MoM) growth percentage.
- Monthly business performance tracking.
